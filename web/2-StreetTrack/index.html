StreetTrack exercise 2

Google's Street View car photographed every street around the Schlachthof. We collect those photos, let a small AI model (running inside this page) look for trucks, and ask Street View for a second photo zoomed in on every truck it finds. Do the steps in order.

After James Bridle, Every CCTV Camera (CC): every CCTV camera on a walk around London's Congestion Charge Zone, from The Nor. Here the camera is Google's, and what we collect are the trucks.

0 Your key

Paste your Google key. This browser remembers it. No key yet? Here is how to get your own (about 15 minutes).

1 Find the Street View panoramas in the area

We put a point every few metres over the area and ask Google where its nearest panorama is (a panorama is one 360° photo). Type a place name, or copy a Box from bboxfinder.com.

The numbers already filled in are the streets around the Schlachthof in Zürich.

2 Download the photos

For every panorama we download 4 photos: looking north, east, south and west.

3 Find the trucks and zoom in

The model (COCO-SSD) knows 80 kinds of things, "truck" is one of them. For every truck it finds, we ask Street View for a new photo pointed at the truck, with a narrow angle: a real zoom.

4 Save the trucks

One .zip with zoom/ (the close-ups), found/ (the original photo with the truck marked in red) and trucks.csv (date, place and direction of every truck).
