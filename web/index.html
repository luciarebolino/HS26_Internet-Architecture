Internet Architecture three scraping exercises

Three small experiments in collecting images from the internet, all around the Schlachthof (slaughterhouse) in Zürich West. Nothing to install: each exercise is a web page that runs in your browser.

ExerciseWhat it doesYou getAfter
13Dminus1 Google's 3D city without the 3D: only the flat textures, as if the buildings were smashed. a map made of textures, and every texture named by its coordinates (JPG) Clement Valla, 3D-Maps-Minus-3D
2StreetTrack Street View photos of the streets around the Schlachthof, searched by an AI model for trucks. a zoomed-in photo of every truck found (JPG), plus where and when James Bridle, Every CCTV Camera (CC)
3Zoooom A tourism webcam's hidden archive: travel through years of photos while zooming into one block. a video (MP4) Sam Lavigne, The Zooms

How to start

  1. Use Google Chrome (other browsers may not make the video in exercise 3).
  2. Open an exercise above. Follow its steps from top to bottom: each button unlocks the next one.
  3. Exercises 1 and 2 need a Google key: from your teacher, or make your own (about 15 minutes). Paste it once, the browser remembers it. Exercise 3 needs no key.
  4. What you make is saved to your Downloads folder (a .zip or an .mp4).
  5. Curious how it works? Open the app.js file in the exercise's folder: the lines starting with // explain each part.

The same exercises also run from the terminal in VS Code: see the README in the main folder.
