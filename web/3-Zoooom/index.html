Zoooom exercise 3

The Zürich West webcam on myswitzerland.com takes a 360° photo of the city every few minutes and keeps them all. The website doesn't show where the photos come from, but the browser's Network tab does: a camera archive at zuerichtourismus.roundshot.com. We read that archive directly, pick a photo for every day, and record a video that travels through time while zooming from Frame A (the city) to Frame B (one far-away block). Do the steps in order.

After Sam Lavigne, The Zooms (2023): every single zooming shot extracted from 600 hours of leaked police helicopter surveillance footage.

1 Open the camera archive

The archive's calendar is a small file listing every day that has photos.

2 Choose which photos

One photo per chosen day, the one closest to the time you pick. Photos before mid-2022 only exist as small previews, so the zoom only gets sharp from June 2022 on. Older photos also download slowly (a few seconds each): start with "every month", it's about 50 photos.

3 Frame A and Frame B

Positions in the full panorama (10240 x 2048 pixels): x and y of the top-left corner, then the width. White = Frame A, red = Frame B. Change the numbers and press Show frames to see them.

4 Record the video

Each photo is shown for a few video frames while the view zooms from A to B over the whole video. Choose one day only and many frames per photo to get a pure zoom on a single moment.
